AdMob Pulse - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

AdMob Pulse ("the app") is a personal AdMob revenue dashboard. This page explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Google Account access

When you sign in, the app requests read-only access to your own AdMob account statistics (the admob.readonly scope) using Google's standard OAuth 2.0 sign-in. This is used only to display your own ad revenue, impressions, clicks and related metrics inside the app. The app cannot modify your AdMob account, ad units, or payment settings, and cannot access any other Google account data.

The resulting access/refresh tokens are stored only on your device, encrypted using the Android Keystore. They are never sent to any server other than Google's own accounts/AdMob API endpoints, and are removed from the device when you sign out.

App lock (PIN / biometrics)

If you enable the app lock, biometric verification (fingerprint/face) is handled entirely by your device's operating system - the app never receives or stores raw biometric data. If you set a PIN instead, only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is stored on your device; the PIN itself is never stored or transmitted anywhere.

Notifications

If you enable the daily summary notification, the app schedules a local notification showing yesterday's estimated earnings. This does not use any push-notification or analytics service - it is generated entirely on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads via the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Google's SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Partner Sites policy and the Google Privacy Policy. The app itself does not separately collect or sell this data.

Data we do not collect

The app has no user accounts of its own, no chat or social features, does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone, and does not use any third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK beyond what is inherent to Google Play Services / Google Mobile Ads.

Data deletion

Signing out of the app removes all locally stored session data (tokens, cached revenue snapshot). Uninstalling the app removes everything else it stored on your device (PIN hash, settings).
